RAHIB'S WORLD

“Some people don’t just grow with time... they grow through pain, silence, and responsibilities.”

INTRODUCTION

WELCOME TO RAHIB’S WORLD—A PLACE WHERE LIFE IS NOT PERFECT, BUT REAL. THIS IS NOT JUST ABOUT STUDIES, BUT ABOUT STRUGGLES, RESPONSIBILITIES, AND THE SILENT BATTLES I FIGHT EVERY DAY. BEING A STUDENT OF ACCOUNTING AND FINANCE, I AM TRYING TO BUILD MY FUTURE, EVEN WHEN LIFE FEELS HEAVY.

My Study Journey

My study journey has not been simple. Like many students, I once had a dream—to become a doctor and study MBBS. It was not just a career choice, but a passion and a goal I truly believed in.

However, after my father’s death, everything changed. My financial situation became difficult, and pursuing MBBS was no longer possible for me. The fees were too high, and without support, it was not affordable. Getting admission on merit also became very tough, and sometimes the system felt unfair.

“My father was not just my parent, he was my strength—and losing him felt like losing the support behind all my dreams.”

Because of these realities, I had to change my path. It was not an easy decision, but I chose to move into Accounting and Finance. At first, it felt like I was leaving my dream behind, but slowly I started accepting it as part of my journey.

From Dream to Determination

Even though I could not become a doctor, I have not stopped dreaming. I realized that success is not about one specific field, but about how strong you stay when life changes your direction.

Today, my goal is to succeed in Accounting and Finance, support my family, and build a stable future. The dream may have changed, but the hard work, passion, and desire to make my father proud are still the same. In fact, now I work even harder because I know I am not just chasing success—I am carrying responsibilities and turning my pain into purpose.

MY SILENT BATTLE

FOR THE PAST TWO YEARS, I HAVE BEEN FIGHTING ANXIETY AND DEPRESSION. THERE WERE DAYS WHEN I FELT COMPLETELY LOST, MENTALLY TIRED, AND BROKEN FROM INSIDE. IT IS NOT EASY TO EXPLAIN THESE FEELINGS TO OTHERS.

AFTER MY FATHER’S DEATH, LIFE CHANGED COMPLETELY. LIFE SUDDENLY BECAME SERIOUS. ALONG WITH EMOTIONAL PAIN, I HAD TO FACE FINANCIAL PROBLEMS AND RESPONSIBILITIES AT A YOUNG AGE. AT TIMES, IT FELT LIKE LIFE WAS TESTING ME BEYOND MY LIMITS.

THERE WERE MOMENTS WHEN I WANTED TO GIVE UP, BUT SOMETHING INSIDE ME KEPT SAYING: “YOU HAVE TO STAY STRONG.” NOW, I AM SLOWLY LEARNING TO LIVE WITH THESE STRUGGLES AND TURN MY PAIN INTO STRENGTH.

RESPONSIBILITIES AND REALITY OF LIFE

LIFE TAUGHT ME EARLY THAT NOTHING COMES EASY. MANAGING STUDIES ALONG WITH RESPONSIBILITIES IS VERY CHALLENGING. SOMETIMES I FEEL TIRED AND ALONE, BUT I KEEP MOVING FORWARD BECAUSE I KNOW I HAVE NO OTHER OPTION.

THESE HARDSHIPS ARE NOT STOPPING ME—THEY ARE MAKING ME STRONGER, MORE RESPONSIBLE, AND MORE MATURE.

THE ROLE OF SUPPORT

IN THIS JOURNEY, EVEN SMALL SUPPORT MATTERS A LOT. IT HELPS ME STAY POSITIVE WHEN LIFE FEELS HEAVY. TALKING TO SOMEONE, EVEN FOR A SHORT TIME, REDUCES THE WEIGHT ON MY HEART AND REMINDS ME THAT I AM NOT COMPLETELY ALONE.

MY MOTHER HAS PLAYED THE BIGGEST ROLE IN MY LIFE. SHE SACRIFICED MANY THINGS FOR MY FUTURE AND EDUCATION. HER SILENT STRUGGLES AND ENDLESS SUPPORT GIVE ME STRENGTH TO KEEP GOING EVERY DAY.

ALONG WITH HER, MY COUSIN AND MY COUSIN’S FAMILY IN KARACHI HAVE ALSO SUPPORTED ME WITH HONESTY AND CARE. THEIR GUIDANCE AND HELP DURING DIFFICULT TIMES MADE A REAL DIFFERENCE IN MY LIFE. HAVING SUCH GENUINE SUPPORT AROUND ME REMINDS ME THAT KINDNESS STILL EXISTS, EVEN IN TOUGH TIMES.

GROWTH THROUGH PAIN

ALL THESE STRUGGLES ARE CHANGING ME SLOWLY. I AM LEARNING PATIENCE, STRENGTH, AND SELF-CONTROL IN SITUATIONS WHERE I ONCE FELT WEAK. I AM ALSO IMPROVING MY SKILLS LIKE EXCEL, ACCOUNTING, AND FREELANCING SO I CAN BECOME INDEPENDENT AND STAND ON MY OWN FEET IN THE FUTURE.

I AM BEGINNING TO UNDERSTAND THAT REAL GROWTH COMES FROM DIFFICULTIES, NOT COMFORT. EVEN SMALL STEPS EVERY DAY ARE MAKING ME BETTER AND STRONGER.

I BELIEVE PAIN IS NOT THE END—IT IS PART OF BECOMING A STRONGER AND MORE CAPABLE PERSON IN LIFE.

CONCLUSION

RAHIB’S WORLD IS NOT A PERFECT WORLD—IT IS A REAL ONE. A WORLD FULL OF STRUGGLES, EMOTIONS, AND SILENT STRENGTH. EVERY DAY IS A CHALLENGE, BUT ALSO A CHANCE TO GROW.

“ONE DAY, ALL THIS PAIN WILL MAKE SENSE... AND ALL THESE STRUGGLES WILL TURN INTO SUCCESS.”
